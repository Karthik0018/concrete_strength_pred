What Makes a Good Concrete Mix?

Cement

The binding agent that holds everything together. Quality and quantity significantly affect strength.

Water-Cement Ratio

Critical factor affecting strength. Lower ratio typically results in higher strength.

Aggregates

Coarse and fine aggregates provide bulk and stability to the concrete mix.

The History of Concrete

Ancient Origins

The Romans were masters of concrete construction

The earliest known use of concrete dates back to 6500 BCE by the Nabataea traders in Syria. However, it was the Romans who perfected the art of concrete construction around 300 BCE. They used a mixture of lime, volcanic ash, and rock to create structures that still stand today, like the Pantheon and the Colosseum.

Modern Era

The invention of Portland cement revolutionized construction

In 1824, Joseph Aspdin, a British bricklayer, invented Portland cement by heating limestone and clay. This led to the development of modern concrete. Today, concrete is the most widely used man-made material in the world, with approximately 14 billion cubic meters produced annually.
